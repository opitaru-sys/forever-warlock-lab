Forever Warlock Lab

Which Warlock build, rotation and race actually wins in Forever, checked against the beta's own spell numbers instead of copied from guides. Every claim is marked as proven by the model, needing an in-game test, or busted. The calculators below run the same model live, so you can change the assumptions and see what flips.

The verdict

  • Leveling 10 to 33Affliction, instant Corruption first, then wand as your filler. Not Shadow Bolt, not Drain Life yet. Talents barely matter here: every sane build lands within 3% of the best.
  • Leveling 34 to 55Drain Life becomes the filler the moment Soul Siphon is done. Keep leveling Affliction-first.
  • Solo from 56Switch to a Demonic Knowledge splash, 28/23/0. It kills 5 to 7% faster than deep Affliction or the published drain-tank build, and held up when spell power tripled and pet damage halved.
  • Group at 60Destruction built around Incinerate, not Shadow Bolt. 4 to 7% ahead of the published build. Your Succubus decides the rest: sacrifice her under about 8% of your damage, keep her out above that, Demonology only above about 17 to 20%.
  • RaceUndead levels fastest. A Human with a caster sword does the most raid damage. Every racial is worth under 2% of raid damage, so pick the race you like.

    The rule behind all of it

    Forever cut most Warlock spells to about half their Classic base damage and moved the power into spell power scaling. Direct nukes kept Classic's scaling. Damage-over-time spells got a lot more of it.

    Consequence: an instant DoT is worth far more per global cooldown than any nuke, at every gear level. That is why Shadow Bolt is a trap while leveling, why Bane of Doom wins at 60, and why every raid build still keeps Corruption and a Bane up.

    Interactions, tested

    • Proven by math

      Human Sword Specialization works on spells

      Forever's version reads "+2% crit with all spells and attacks while you have a sword equipped", and Warlocks can train swords. With Ruin or Pandemic doubling crit damage, that is 1.6 to 2.2% more raid damage, the biggest racial for a Warlock. It only counts if a caster sword is your best weapon.

    • Proven by math

      Bane of Doom beats Bane of Agony at 60

      Per second, both scale identically with spell power (4.0 over 60 s equals 1.6 over 24 s). Doom has more base damage per second and needs a global cooldown once a minute instead of every 24 seconds. Rule: cast Doom while at least a minute of the fight remains, then Agony. The result rests on the datamined 4.0 scaling. Drag the slider in the raid calculator to see where it flips.

    • Proven by math

      Incinerate beats Shadow Bolt as the Destruction filler

      With Bane 5/5 and Immolate up, Incinerate does 26 to 29% more per second of casting than Shadow Bolt before school buffs, which is 4 to 7% across the whole rotation. Forever reversed the sacrifice buffs from Classic: Imp now gives Shadow, Succubus gives Fire. One risk: if Improved Shadow Bolt lasts 60 seconds instead of 12, the gap closes to about 1%. Try the toggle.

    • Proven by math

      Demonic Knowledge is the best point-for-point talent in the class

      It adds flat spell damage equal to your level, for you and your demon, while a demon is out. Flat bonuses are worth most when gear is weakest, which is solo play. It costs 20 Demonology points of mostly survival talents to reach, so it pays off from about level 56.

    • Proven by math

      Nightfall is underrated once you drain

      It can proc on every Corruption tick and every Drain Life and Wrack tick, once per second while channeling. At 60 it is worth about 3% per point, even after paying the global cooldown for the instant Shadow Bolt.

    • Needs an in-game test

      Stacking sacrifices with Demonic Pact

      Demonic Pact only says a sacrifice is not cancelled by summoning a different demon. Nothing says a second sacrifice removes the first. If they stack, sacrificing the Imp, Felhunter and Voidwalker in turn and keeping the Succubus is about 9% faster solo. That assumes the damage Soul Link moves onto the Succubus costs nothing. If you must heal her back up, it is 13% slower. A curiosity, not a plan.

    • Needs an in-game test

      Do curses count for Soul Siphon?

      Soul Siphon grows Drain Life by 12% per other Affliction effect on the target, up to three. Curses now sit in their own slot next to Banes. If a curse counts, Corruption, a Bane and Curse of Weakness max it out without Siphon Life.

    • Busted

      Shadow Bolt as a leveling filler

      It costs the most mana per damage of anything you have, so the resting it causes cancels the faster kills. It won 0 of 612 level-by-level scans.

    • Busted

      Troll Warlock as a raid pick

      Berserking is casting speed, and in the beta haste does not speed up DoTs or channels. It adds about 0.2% raid damage. Rapid Regeneration is a 6-second channel that breaks on any action, so it is between-fights healing, not a combat heal.

    • Known, confirmed

      Demonic Pact: sacrifice the Imp, then summon the Succubus

      The Demonology raid guide already uses this. It tops the raid calculator once the Succubus carries about 17 to 20% of your damage, or about 8% if your gear caps hit. The BlizzCon demo credited her with 23 to 25% at level 38, so this is a live contender.

    Help test these

    These five checks decide the open questions. Tick them off as you go. Your ticks stay in this browser only.

    • Any level, beta
      Life Tap ratio. Write down health and mana, tap once, compare. Sources disagree on whether it converts 430 plus Spirit or 840. If Spirit adds to it, Human and high-Spirit gear buff every tap.
    • Level 20 and up, beta
      Succubus share. Run a damage meter over five minutes of normal play with the Succubus out. Put her damage per second into the raid calculator above.
    • Level 30, beta cap
      Soul Siphon and curses. With Soul Siphon, compare a Drain Life tick with Corruption and Agony on the target, then again after adding Curse of Weakness. A 12% jump means curses count.
    • Level 40, after launch
      Pact stacking. With 31 Demonology points, sacrifice the Imp, summon and sacrifice the Felhunter, then check your buff bar for both buffs.
    • Level 60, after launch
      Bane of Doom scaling. Cast it on a training dummy in two gear sets with different spell power. The hit should move by four times the spell power difference.

    How this was checked

    Spell data came from Wowhead's Forever spell pages, including per-tick spell power scaling, cross-checked against two beta client datamines, ForeverChanges and the ElliotWood Forever sim. Talent values came from the Wowhead calculator's rank tooltips, cross-checked against five talent sites. Racial values came from Wowhead's Forever spell data.

    Leveling runs through an expected-value fight simulator. It folds hit and crit into every tick, applies buffs live because Forever removed snapshotting, ramps Agony the way the tooltip says, enforces talent row requirements, and scores seconds per kill including rest. A hill-climbing optimizer searched both trees at levels 20 to 60. The raid calculator is a steady-state spell-budget model that balances mana with Life Tap.

    The research, models and this page were built with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. A separate Claude session then reviewed the work adversarially, starting from its own code: it rebuilt the raid math in its own script plus a turn-by-turn rotation simulation. The two agreed within 0.3%. It confirmed every headline with caveats, corrected three thresholds and one unfair comparison, and checked the solo claims under tripled spell power and halved pet damage. The calculator on this page is a direct port of the reviewed model and reproduces it exactly across 54 test cases.

    Limits. The beta is capped at level 30, so nobody has played a finished tree. Pet damage, mob health and damage, wand damage and rest speed are assumptions that were stress-tested, not measured. The raid model ignores mana regen, potions and raid buffs, which would help Destruction and Demonology a little more than Affliction. Numbers will move before 4 November. Corrections are welcome.
