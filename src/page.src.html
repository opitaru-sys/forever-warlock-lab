The short answer

New to Warlocks? You win by attrition. You stack damage-over-time spells (DoTs) on a target, let a demon pet tank or fight, and trade your own health for mana with Life Tap, then drain the health back. Forever made this style stronger: its DoTs scale with your gear far better than your direct nukes do.

Glossary of the terms on this page
DoT
Damage over time. A spell that keeps hurting the target for a while, like Corruption.
Filler
What you cast while your DoTs are ticking: wand, Drain Life, Shadow Bolt or Incinerate.
Wand
A ranged weapon that shoots for free. Early on it is your best filler.
Spell power (SP)
The gear stat that makes every spell hit harder.
GCD
Global cooldown. The 1.5 seconds after most spells before you can cast the next one.
Proc
A random bonus that triggers on its own, like Nightfall's free instant Shadow Bolt.
Sacrifice
Demonic Sacrifice: kill your own demon for a 2-hour buff. In Forever the Imp gives Shadow damage and the Succubus gives Fire damage.
Hit cap
Against raid bosses, 16% hit stops your spells missing. Below it, each missing 1% costs about 1% of damage.
28/23/0
Talent points in Affliction, Demonology and Destruction, in that order.
Bane and Curse
Forever split them into two slots, so you can keep one Bane and one Curse on the same target.
Drain tank
Leveling by letting your pet hold the mob while Drain Life heals you.

          Why DoTs beat Shadow Bolt in Forever

          Forever cut most Warlock spells to about half their Classic base damage and moved the power into spell power scaling. Direct nukes kept Classic's scaling. DoTs got a lot more of it.

          Claims we checked

          Checked by the model · 6

          Undead Touch of the Grave is the biggest raid racial

          Casters get their own version (Wowhead Forever spell 1260201, for Priest, Mage and Warlock): each spell or attack has a 10% chance to drain 5% of your max health from the target, at most once per second. Warriors, Paladins and Rogues get a 5% version. A raid Warlock makes about half a damaging cast per second, so at 4,500 health that is about 10 damage per second, 1.6 to 2.3% of raid damage depending on your health. The model counts casts only. If DoT ticks can trigger it too, it roughly doubles. A reader caught this: the page first used the 5% version. This is test 7.

          Human Sword Specialization works on spells

          Forever's version reads "+2% crit with all spells and attacks while you have a sword equipped", and Warlocks can train swords. With Ruin or Pandemic doubling crit damage, that is 1.6 to 2.2% more raid damage, one of the two biggest racials for a Warlock next to Undead's Touch of the Grave. It only counts if a caster sword is your best weapon.

          Bane of Doom beats Bane of Agony at 60

          Per second, both scale identically with spell power (4.0 over 60 s equals 1.6 over 24 s). Doom has more base damage per second and needs a global cooldown once a minute instead of every 24 seconds. Rule: cast Doom while at least a minute of the fight remains, then Agony. The result rests on the datamined 4.0 scaling. Drag the Bane of Doom slider to see where it flips.

          Incinerate beats Shadow Bolt as the Destruction filler

          With Bane 5/5 and Immolate up, Incinerate does 26 to 29% more per second of casting than Shadow Bolt before school buffs, which is 4 to 7% across the whole rotation. Forever reversed the sacrifice buffs from Classic: Imp now gives Shadow, Succubus gives Fire. One risk: if Improved Shadow Bolt lasts 60 seconds instead of 12, the gap closes to about 1%. Try the toggle.

          Demonic Knowledge is the best point-for-point talent in the class

          It adds flat spell damage equal to your level, for you and your demon, while a demon is out. Flat bonuses are worth most when gear is weakest, which is solo play. It costs 20 Demonology points of mostly survival talents to reach, so it pays off from about level 56.

          Nightfall is underrated once you drain

          It can proc on every Corruption tick and every Drain Life and Wrack tick, once per second while channeling. At 60 it is worth about 3% per point, even after paying the global cooldown for the instant Shadow Bolt.

          Needs an in-game test · 3

          A Succubus leveling build, if she can hold aggro

          A 5/31/0 build with the Imp sacrificed through Demonic Pact and the Succubus out ties the Affliction route at 45 and kills 4 to 7% faster from 50. The swing is who takes the hits. If the Succubus holds the mob, it becomes about 15 to 19% faster than anything else tested, because your resting drops away. Demonic Brand is built for exactly that: Searing Pain brands the target, and your pet's next 3 attacks on it generate high threat. The Voidwalker is a bit slower in this build and the Felhunter slowest. Suggested by a reader. This is test 6.

          Stacking sacrifices with Demonic Pact

          Demonic Pact only says a sacrifice is not cancelled by summoning a different demon. Nothing says a second sacrifice removes the first. If they stack, sacrificing the Imp, Felhunter and Voidwalker in turn and keeping the Succubus is about 9% faster solo. That assumes the damage Soul Link moves onto the Succubus costs nothing. If you must heal her back up, it is 13% slower. A curiosity, not a plan. This is test 4.

          Do curses count for Soul Siphon?

          Soul Siphon grows Drain Life by 12% per other Affliction effect on the target, up to three. Curses now sit in their own slot next to Banes. If a curse counts, Corruption, a Bane and Curse of Weakness max it out without Siphon Life. This is test 3.

          Busted · 2

          Shadow Bolt as a leveling filler

          It costs the most mana per damage of anything you have, so the resting it causes cancels the faster kills. It won 0 of 612 level-by-level scans.

          Troll Warlock as a raid pick

          Berserking is casting speed, and in the beta haste does not speed up DoTs or channels. It adds about 0.2% raid damage. Rapid Regeneration is a 6-second channel that breaks on any action or damage, so in PvE it is between-fights healing. In PvP it shines behind a Fear: the target runs, you channel 50% of your health back.

          Known and confirmed · 1

          Demonic Pact: sacrifice the Imp, then summon the Succubus

          The wowforeverbuilds Demonology guide already uses this. It tops the raid calculator once the Succubus carries about 17 to 20% of your damage, or about 8% if your gear caps hit. The BlizzCon demo credited her with 23 to 25% at level 38, so this is a live contender.

          How this was checked

          Limits. The beta is capped at level 30, so nobody has played a finished tree. Pet damage, mob health and damage, wand damage and rest speed are assumptions that were stress-tested, not measured. The raid model ignores mana regen, potions and raid buffs, which would help Destruction and Demonology a little more than Affliction. Numbers will move before 4 November. Corrections are welcome.

          Method, data and review

          Spell data came from Wowhead's Forever spell pages, including per-tick spell power scaling, cross-checked against two beta client datamines, ForeverChanges and the ElliotWood Forever sim. Talent values came from the Wowhead calculator's rank tooltips, cross-checked against five talent sites. Racial values came from Wowhead's Forever spell data. Dungeon levels came from zockify.com and lfcarry.com.

          Leveling runs through an expected-value fight simulator. It folds hit and crit into every tick, applies buffs live because Forever removed snapshotting, ramps Agony the way the tooltip says, enforces talent row requirements, and scores seconds per kill including rest. A hill-climbing optimizer searched both trees at levels 20 to 60. The raid calculator is a steady-state spell-budget model that balances mana with Life Tap.

          The research, models and this page were built with Claude, Anthropic's AI model. A separate Claude session then reviewed the work adversarially, starting from its own code: it rebuilt the raid math in its own script plus a turn-by-turn rotation simulation. The two agreed within 0.3%. It confirmed every headline with caveats, corrected three thresholds and one unfair comparison, and checked the solo claims under tripled spell power and halved pet damage. The calculator on this page is a direct port of the reviewed model and reproduces it exactly across 54 test cases.
